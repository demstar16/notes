## Copilot Studio
A credit refers to how much it costs to perform a certain action within the platform

| **Feature**            | **Billing Rate**   | **What is it?**                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Classic Answer         | 1 Copilot Credit   | Any response that doesn't require searching other places (instructions, uploaded file, etc.)                                                 |
| Generative Answer      | 2 Copilot Credits  | Where answers involve the agents general knowledge and/or internet as a source of retrieval.                                                 |
| Agent Action           | 5 Copilot Credits  | Where the copilot has to search through your Dataverse layout or SharePoint. Anything to do with direct retrieval through your MS resources. |
| Tenant Graph Grounding | 10 Copilot Credits | Where copilot searches through all of your MS areas automatically (Work IQ).                                                                 |
## AI Builder / AI Tools
A token is approximately one word.

| **Feature**             | **Model**             | **Text rate per unit**                                    |
| ----------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Basic                   | GPT4.1 Mini           | 0.1 Copilot Credit per 1K tokens, or 1 image, or 1 page.  |
| Standard                | GPT-4.1 or GPT-5 chat | 1.5 Copilot Credits per 1K tokens, or 1 image, or 1 page. |
| Premium                 | GPT-5 reasoning       | 10 Copilot Credits per 1K tokens, or 1 image, or 1 page.  |
| Content processing tool |                       | 8 Copilot Credits per image                               |
